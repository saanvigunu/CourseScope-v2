Class #:19286
Units:4

This course explores the unique and insightful story of the Israeli economy. We begin with an overview of Israel’s transformation from a relatively poor region in the Middle East a century ago into a modern, high-income economy recognized for its global leadership in technology and innovation. The course then focuses on the central transitions that have shaped the contemporary Israeli economy, addressing both its notable achievements and enduring challenges. Through these themes, the course aims to draw broader insights into macroeconomic theory and policy analysis by using the Israeli experience as a case study.
