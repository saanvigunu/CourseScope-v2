Course Catalog Description

Foundations of data science from three perspectives: inferential thinking, computational thinking, and real-world relevance. Given data arising from some real-world phenomenon, how does one analyze that data so as to understand that phenomenon? The course teaches critical concepts and skills in computer programming and statistical inference, in conjunction with hands-on analysis of real-world datasets, including economic data, document collections, geographical data, and social networks. It delves into social and legal issues surrounding data analysis, including issues of privacy and data ownership.

There will be 2 ways to enroll in Data C8 in Spring 2027:

Lec 001 will be scheduled in-person. Students enrolled in Lec 001 should plan to attend in-person at the scheduled time. All seats will initially be reserved for new first-year students (1-2 Terms in Attendance on CalCentral)*

Lec 002 will be scheduled at the same time as the in-person lecture, but students in Lec 002 cannot attend lecture in-person at the regularly scheduled time during the first two weeks of classes. Instead, students enrolled in Lec 002 are expected to watch the recorded lecture, or (if available) attend lecture online at the scheduled time. Instructors will announce when all students are invited to attend lecture in person. Lecture recordings will be available all semester. Online lectures will be available as long as needed to ensure that the in-person lecture hall remains below its capacity and all students who plan to attend lecture at the scheduled time can do so. Enrollment in Lec 002 will be open to all students.

*If you are not a new first-year student but have a compelling reason why you need to enroll in Lec 001 in-person, please email ds-advising@berkeley.edu with additional details about your situation.

Lab Sections: All students will enroll in a 999 lab on CalCentral. All students enrolled in either Lec 001 or Lec 002 will sign up for a 2-hour in-person lab section, which will be held 12pm or later on Wednesdays, throughout the day on Thursdays, or Friday mornings. Specific lab assignments are determined once instruction begins.

Time Conflicts: Data C8 does not allow enrollment with time conflicts.

Exams: All exams for Data C8 will be in-person. There will be an out-of-class midterm, and a final exam. The final exam will be held on Monday, 5/10 from 3:00-6:00pm. This class will offer an alternate final exam on Monday, 5/10 from 7:00-10:00pm, but only for students who have a course conflict with the regular final exam time or who cannot attend the regular final exam due to a DSP accommodation. show less
